How it works

  1. Your info — enter your name, emails, phones, addresses. Stored only in your browser.
  2. Delete all — a guided walk-through of every enabled broker that isn't confirmed yet. Each step copies your details to the clipboard and opens the broker's official opt-out page (or a prefilled email where the broker takes email requests).
  3. Track it — mark each one Submitted, then Confirmed once you click the broker's verification email. A re-check date is set automatically (30 days after submitting, 90 days after confirming).
  4. Re-check weekly — brokers re-list people all the time. Download the weekly Monday 9 AM calendar reminder and come back here; anything due shows up as "Re-check due".

What it doesn't do

  • It can't submit forms on other websites for you — browsers don't allow that, and most brokers require a captcha or email verification you must complete yourself.
  • It never sends your info anywhere. There is no backend. Opt-out pages open with no referrer.
  • Opt-out links change; if one is broken, search the broker's site for "opt out" or "do not sell".
